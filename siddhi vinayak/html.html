process

High-Quality

1. Cultivation & Harvesting Makhana plants grow in shallow ponds and wetlands. When the fruits mature, seeds are collected manually from the pond bottom by trained workers.


2. Washing & Sun Drying Freshly harvested seeds are thoroughly washed to remove mud and impurities. Seeds are then sun-dried for several days to reduce moisture and harden the shell.


3. Grading (Raw Seed Sorting) Dried seeds are sorted by size and quality. Uniform sizing helps ensure even roasting and better popping.


4. Roasting (First Heating) Seeds are roasted in iron pans over controlled heat. This loosens the hard outer shell and prepares the kernel for popping.


5. Popping (Traditional Technique) Roasted seeds are cracked and reheated. With precise heat and timing, the inner kernel pops, turning white and fluffy — this is makhana.


6. Polishing & Cleaning Popped makhana is rubbed and cleaned to remove remaining husk. Broken or under-expanded pieces are removed.


7. Final Grading & Packaging Makhana is graded again based on size, whiteness, and texture. It is packed in moisture-proof packaging to maintain freshness and shelf life.
